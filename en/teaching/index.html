“AI in Marketing” course

I wrote this programme for AlmaU, from LLM basics to end-to-end analytics and building a website with AI. Students do each assignment by hand first, then check it with AI.

  1. AI in marketing demystified: model types, LLMs, prompting, tools
  2. AI, SEO and GEO: optimising for search engines and generative answers
  3. AI in marketing analytics and forecasting
  4. End-to-end analytics: ads → website → CRM → sales, ROAS and CAC
  5. Vibe coding: from competitor research to a live website
  6. Marketing funnel: building and stress-testing it with AI
