How I work: Revanite Growth Formula

  1. Insight — understanding the customer, the market and the real reasons to buy
  2. Economics — unit economics: CAC, LTV, margin, payback
  3. Strategy — choice of segments, channels and value proposition
  4. Hypotheses — a prioritized testing plan
  5. Execution — fast launch at minimal cost
  6. Analytics — end-to-end, from first touch to payment in the CRM
  7. Scale — invest in what pays back, drop what does not

How is a startup tracker different from a marketing contractor?

A tracker does not do the work for the team: a tracker helps find the key constraint on growth, frame hypotheses, test them quickly and economically, and keep the team focused on metrics. A contractor implements the chosen solution. Ruslan works in both roles: tracking through Revanite Tracking and tech hubs, implementation through Revanite Studio, including sales, CRM and automation.
