Marketing Management · Core practice

Managing your business's marketing

We act as the company's external marketing partner and take on the systematic management of marketing — from analysis and strategy to implementation control and regular analytics.

Discuss a projectTelegram

What's included

  • Analysis of current marketing and business objectives
  • Marketing strategy development
  • Target audience and positioning
  • Marketing plan
  • Planning of ad channels and budgets
  • Briefing and managing marketers, designers, contractors and other specialists
  • Control over marketing activities
  • Analysis of acquisition channel performance
  • Sales funnel and lead quality
  • Regular analytics and growth recommendations

Who it's for

  • The business has no in-house head of marketing
  • The owner manages marketers and contractors personally but wants to delegate this function
  • The company has marketers but no shared strategy or management system
  • The business wants to grow its marketing but is not ready to hire a head of marketing yet

Result

The business gains an external strategic partner who manages marketing and ties marketing actions to business goals.

Format: Ongoing monthly engagement with weekly reporting.

Ads, websites and analytics are implemented by the Digital Growth practice. If the challenge goes beyond marketing and concerns the business as a whole, Business Tracking is the right fit.
